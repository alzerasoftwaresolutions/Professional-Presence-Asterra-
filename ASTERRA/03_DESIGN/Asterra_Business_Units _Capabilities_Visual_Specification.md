# 1. Purpose

The Business Units / Capabilities page should answer:

> **What does Asterra do, and in which areas does it operate?**

This is the primary **capability discovery page**.

It should allow visitors to quickly:

* Understand Asterra's areas of operation
* Compare business units
* Identify the capability relevant to them
* Enter a deeper Business Unit Detail page

The page should be **structured and authoritative**, but not feel like a software feature directory.

# 4. Hero Visual Direction

The Business page should feel slightly more **structured and energetic** than the About page.

Visual emphasis:

```text id="7fl2cr"

→ Narrative

Business
→ Structure + Capability

Projects
→ Evidence
```

This differentiation should happen through composition rather than introducing a completely different design language.

# 5. Capability Introduction

A short introduction should explain how Asterra organizes its capabilities.

Recommended:


# 8. Business Unit Visual Hierarchy

Every unit should communicate:

```text id="z7oqf7"
01
Business Unit Name
        ↓
Short positioning statement
        ↓
Key capabilities / scope
        ↓
Explore →
```

The name should dominate.

The supporting description should remain concise.

---

# 9. Business Unit Imagery

Images are optional.

Use imagery when it helps distinguish the business unit.

Good examples:

* Relevant project
* Industry environment
* Facility
* Equipment
* Real team activity

Avoid assigning arbitrary stock images to every capability.

A page with five generic images is weaker than a page with two meaningful ones.

# 20. Responsive Behavior

### Desktop

Business units can use:

* Two-column grid
* Editorial list
* Image/text split

### Tablet

Reduce complexity while maintaining clear hierarchy.

### Mobile

Stack business units:

```text id="y0q1x9"
01
Business Unit
Description
Explore →

02
Business Unit
Description
Explore →

03
Business Unit
Description
Explore →
```

Do not compress multiple capabilities into narrow columns.

---

# 21. Mobile Interaction

Every business unit should expose its navigation clearly.

Avoid hover-dependent content.

If the desktop design uses image overlays, all essential information must still be visible or accessible on mobile.

---

# 22. Business Unit Detail Transition

The visual language should clearly indicate that each listing item leads to a deeper page.

```text id="6x6jvw"
Business Listing
       ↓
Business Unit Detail
       ↓
Capabilities
       ↓
Projects / Evidence
       ↓
Contact
```

This creates a natural information hierarchy.

---

# 23. Internal Linking

The page should link naturally to:

* Business Unit Detail pages
* Relevant Projects
* About / Corporate Story
* Contact

Avoid linking every business unit to every other page.

Links should represent genuine relationships.

---

# 24. SEO Structure

Recommended:

```text id="a5e3iy"
H1
Business / Capabilities

H2
Business Units

H3
Business Unit A

H3
Business Unit B

H3

```

Each business unit should have descriptive link text.

Avoid keyword stuffing in capability descriptions.

---

# 25. Metadata

The page should have:

* Unique title
* Useful meta description
* Correct canonical
* Appropriate social metadata

Individual Business Unit Detail pages should have their own unique metadata.

---

# 26. Structured Data

Where relevant, business units may correspond to service or organizational information.

Structured data should accurately represent visible page content.

Do not create artificial structured data simply because a Schema.org type exists.

---

# 27. Image SEO

For capability imagery:

* Descriptive filenames
* Accurate alt text
* Responsive sizing
* Appropriate image format
* Reserved dimensions
* Lazy loading below the fold

Decorative imagery should not be stuffed with keywords.

---

# 28. Performance

The capability page should avoid becoming image-heavy.

Prioritize:

* Responsive images
* Efficient formats
* Limited animations
* Appropriate lazy loading
* Minimal unnecessary JavaScript

If many capabilities exist, do not load every large image at full resolution immediately.

---

# 29. Accessibility

Required:

* Logical heading hierarchy
* Keyboard-accessible links
* Visible focus states
* Sufficient contrast
* Descriptive link labels
* Meaningful alt text
* Accessible interactive elements

The numbered structure must not be the only way users understand the order.

---

# 30. Motion

Recommended:

```text id="o4b5c8"
Business unit hover
→ subtle emphasis

Image
→ restrained transition

Arrow
→ small directional movement
```

Avoid:

* Large card rotations
* Excessive zoom
* Complex scroll effects
* Animated numbering
* Continuous motion

---

# 31. Content Integrity

Business unit information must reflect the actual organization.

Do not invent:

* Capabilities
* Divisions
* Services
* Geographic operations
* Certifications
* Industry specializations

If Asterra has only a small number of business areas, the page should remain appropriately focused.

# 34. Quality Gate

Before implementation approval:

### Clarity

* Can a visitor understand what Asterra does quickly?

### Differentiation

* Can each business unit be distinguished?

### Hierarchy

* Is the primary capability structure obvious?

### Navigation

* Can users easily enter deeper business pages?

### Evidence

* Are capabilities connected to genuine projects where useful?

### Responsive

* Does the listing remain easy to scan on mobile?

### SEO

* Is each capability clearly represented semantically?

### Performance

* Are images and interactions lightweight?

### Authenticity

* Does every capability reflect verified Asterra information?

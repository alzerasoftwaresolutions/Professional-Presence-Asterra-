# 1. Purpose

The About page should answer one central question:

> **Who is Asterra, what does it stand for, and how has it developed into the organization it is today?**

The page should communicate **identity and institutional credibility**, not primarily sell individual services.

Its visual character should therefore be more editorial and narrative than the Business section.

---

# 2. Overall Composition

```text
ABOUT / CORPORATE STORY
│
├── Global Header
│
├── Corporate Story Hero
│
├── Who We Are
│
├── Asterra Story / History
│
├── Mission / Vision / Values
│
├── How We Work / Corporate Approach
│
├── Leadership Introduction
│
├── Business / Capability Transition
│
└── Global Footer
```

The page should feel like a continuous corporate narrative rather than a collection of independent modules.

---

# 3. Hero

## Visual Objective

Immediately establish the page as the **corporate identity** section.

Recommended composition:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                                      │
│  ABOUT ASTERRA                                       │
│                                                      │
│  Asterra's corporate story                           │
│  presented as a strong editorial statement.          │
│                                                      │
│                                  Large image         │
│                                                      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The hero should be quieter than the homepage hero.

The objective is **context**, not maximum promotional impact.

---

# 4. Hero Image

Preferred imagery:

* Asterra facility
* Major project environment
* Corporate headquarters
* Leadership/team
* Relevant industry environment

The image should communicate the scale and character of the organization.

Avoid generic business stock photography.

---

# 5. Hero Typography

Hierarchy:

```text
ABOUT ASTERRA
      ↓
Primary page heading
      ↓
Short supporting statement
```

The H1 should clearly communicate the page topic.

The page should not compete typographically with the homepage's main brand statement.

---

# 6. Who We Are

## Purpose

Provide the core corporate introduction.

Recommended composition:

```text
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ WHO WE ARE                                           │
│                                                      │
│ Large statement                 Supporting text      │
│                                                      │
│                                 Optional image       │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The large statement should communicate Asterra's identity.

Supporting text provides the necessary context.

---

# 7. Editorial Layout

Use a strong text/image relationship.

Possible arrangement:

```text
Text
████████████████

                 Image
                 ███████████
                 ███████████
```

or:

```text
Image             Text
████████          ████████
████████          ████████
```

The layout can alternate throughout the page.

---

# 8. Story / History

## Purpose

Show how Asterra developed.

A timeline can be used if the organization has meaningful historical milestones.

Example:

```text
ASTERRA'S JOURNEY

● Foundation
│
│
● Major development
│
│
● Expansion
│
│
● Current position
```

Do not create a timeline merely because timelines look visually attractive.

If Asterra's story is better communicated as prose, use an editorial narrative instead.

---

# 9. Timeline Visual Treatment

If a timeline is used:

* Keep it simple
* Prioritize dates and milestones
* Avoid excessive icons
* Use typography and spacing for hierarchy
* Keep the visual line subtle

Example:

```text
2020
Foundation
Short explanation

2022
Major milestone
Short explanation

2025
Expansion
Short explanation
```

The timeline should remain readable on mobile.

# 11. Values

Values should not become generic icon cards.

Preferred structure:

```text
01
Integrity

Short explanation.

02
Excellence

Short explanation.

03

Short explanation.
```

Use actual Asterra values from the approved content.

Do not invent values.

---

# 12. Corporate Approach

## Purpose

Explain **how Asterra operates**, not simply what it sells.

Possible themes:

* Professional standards
* Collaboration
* Technical expertise
* Quality
* Responsibility
* Long-term thinking

Visual approach:

```text
APPROACH

Large statement

Supporting explanation

01 — Principle
02 — Principle
03 — Principle
```

This section can visually bridge the About page to the Business section.

---

# 13. Leadership Introduction

The About page should introduce leadership without duplicating the Leadership page.

Recommended:

```text
OUR LEADERSHIP

Short introductory statement

┌────────────┐ ┌────────────┐ ┌────────────┐
│ Portrait   │ │ Portrait   │ │ Portrait   │
│ Name       │ │ Name       │ │ Name       │
│ Role       │ │ Role       │ │ Role       │
└────────────┘ └────────────┘ └────────────┘

Meet the leadership →
```

Only selected executives should appear.

The complete leadership team belongs on the Leadership listing.

---

# 14. Leadership Photography

Portraits should feel consistent.

Maintain:

* Similar crop
* Similar aspect ratio
* Similar visual treatment
* Consistent image quality

Avoid mixing dramatically different portrait styles.

---

# 15. Business Transition

The About page should eventually point toward what Asterra actually does.

Recommended:

```text
FROM WHO WE ARE
        ↓
TO WHAT WE DO

Explore Asterra's business areas →
```

This should be a transition, not a second Business section.

---

# 16. Final CTA

The About page can close with a dual-purpose CTA.

Example structure:

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ DISCOVER ASTERRA                              │
│                                               │
│ Explore our capabilities and experience.     │
│                                               │
│ [Explore Business] [View Projects]            │
```

The exact copy should come from the approved content.

# 17. Visual Rhythm

The About page should use a narrative rhythm:

```text
Hero
 ↓
Identity
 ↓
Story
 ↓
Foundation
 ↓
Approach
 ↓
People
 ↓
Capabilities
```

This creates a natural progression:

**Who → Where we came from → What we believe → How we work → Who leads us → What we do**

---

# 18. Alternating Editorial Layouts

Avoid repeating the same layout.

For example:

```text
Section 01
Text | Image

Section 02
Image | Text

Section 03
Full-width statement

Section 04
Structured values

Section 05
People grid
```

This provides visual variation while preserving a consistent grid.

---

# 19. Full-Width Statement

At least one section may use a large full-width corporate statement.

Example:

```text
┌───────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                               │
│ “A large approved Asterra statement           │
│  can occupy the visual field here.”           │
│                                               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Use only approved corporate messaging.

This section should create visual breathing room.

---

# 20. Image Strategy

The About page should use fewer but more meaningful images than a portfolio page.

Prioritize:

1. Corporate environment
2. People
3. Historical moments
4. Projects that communicate organizational identity

Each image should support the story.

---

# 21. Image Treatment

Favor:

* Large editorial crops
* Natural photography
* Strong rectangular compositions
* Controlled aspect ratios

Avoid:

* Excessive rounded cards
* Decorative frames
* Heavy filters
* Generic stock-photo collages

---

# 22. Typography

The About page should make stronger use of editorial typography.

Suggested hierarchy:

```text
Eyebrow
     ↓
Large narrative statement
     ↓
Section heading
     ↓
Body copy
```

Large statements can be significantly larger than standard body headings when they communicate an important corporate idea.

---

# 23. Content Width

Long corporate descriptions should not span the entire screen.

Use:

```text
Large statement
──────────────
Controlled width

Supporting text
──────────────
Readable width
```

This improves readability and creates stronger visual hierarchy.

---

# 24. Mobile Composition

Desktop:

```text
Text       Image
```

Mobile:

```text
Text
Image
```

For alternating sections:

```text
Desktop:
Image | Text
Text  | Image

Mobile:
Image
Text

Text
Image
```

The semantic reading order must remain logical.

---

# 25. Mobile Timeline

If the history section uses a timeline:

```text
2020

   │
2022
Milestone
   │
2025
Expansion
```

Do not attempt to preserve a complex horizontal desktop timeline on small screens.

# 26. Mobile Values

Desktop:

```text

```

Mobile:

```text

```

Each value should remain readable without excessive card decoration.

# 27. Navigation

The About page should clearly indicate its position in the global navigation.

The active navigation state should be visible but subtle.

Breadcrumbs are optional depending on the final site navigation depth.

---

# 28. Internal Links

Important contextual links:

```text
About
```

Leadership section:

```text

   ↓
Leadership Listing
```

Capabilities transition:

```text
About
   ↓
```

# 29. SEO Considerations

The page should have a clear corporate identity topic.

Recommended semantic structure:

```text
H1
About Asterra / Corporate Story

H2
Who We Are

H2
Our Story

H2
Mission, Vision & Values

H2
Our Approach

H2
Leadership
```

Exact headings should reflect the final approved copy.

Do not create multiple H1 elements merely for visual styling.

---

# 30. Structured Data

Where appropriate, the page can support organization-related structured data.

The information must correspond to actual visible and verified corporate information.

Do not add unrelated Schema.org types simply to increase markup volume.

---

# 31. Image SEO

Meaningful corporate images should have:

* Descriptive filenames
* Accurate alt text
* Responsive sizing
* Appropriate formats
* Reserved dimensions

Decorative imagery should not receive keyword-stuffed alt text.

---

# 32. Performance

The About page may contain several large images.

Prioritize:

* Responsive image delivery
* Correct dimensions
* Lazy loading for below-fold images
* Efficient formats
* Limited animation
* Controlled font loading

The hero image should receive special loading consideration because it contributes to above-the-fold rendering.

---

# 33. Motion

Motion should reinforce the narrative.

Possible:

```text
Story section
→ subtle reveal

Timeline
→ restrained progression

Image
→ subtle hover treatment

CTA
→ simple interaction
```

Avoid animating every section independently.

The page should still communicate its complete story when animations are disabled.

---

# 34. Accessibility

Required:

* Logical heading hierarchy
* Keyboard navigation
* Visible focus states
* Accessible navigation
* Meaningful image alternatives
* Readable contrast
* Proper link labels
* Reduced-motion support

The timeline, if used, must remain understandable without relying on visual positioning alone.

---

# 35. Content Integrity

The visual design must not encourage fabricated corporate history.

Do not create:

* Unverified founding dates
* Invented milestones
* Unsupported statistics
* Fake certifications
* Generic claims presented as facts

If information is unavailable, simplify the section.

# 37. Relationship to Existing Pages

The About page should act as an entry point into deeper areas.

```text
ABOUT
 │
 ├── Leadership ─────→ Executive Profiles
 │
 ├── Business ───────→ Business Details
 │
 └── Projects ───────→ Project Details
```

It should **introduce**, not replace, those sections.

---

# 38. Quality Gate

Before implementation approval:

### Identity

* Does the page communicate who Asterra is?

### Story

* Is the narrative easy to follow?

### Visual hierarchy

* Are major corporate ideas visually dominant?

### Authenticity

* Are images and claims credible?

### Differentiation

* Does it feel editorial rather than generic?

### Navigation

* Can users move naturally into Leadership, Business, and Projects?

### Mobile

* Does the narrative remain clear on small screens?

### SEO

* Is the page semantically structured?

### Performance

* Are large images handled responsibly?

# 39. Final Visual Direction

The About page should feel:

```text
                    ASTERRA
                       │
                       ↓
                  WHO WE ARE
                       │
                       ↓
                  OUR STORY
                       │
                       ↓
              WHAT WE STAND FOR
                       │
                       ↓
                HOW WE WORK
                       │
                       ↓
                 OUR PEOPLE
                       │
                       ↓
                WHAT WE DO
```

The dominant design language should be:

**Editorial + Institutional + Human + Precise**

rather than:

**Promotional + Card-heavy + Decorative.**

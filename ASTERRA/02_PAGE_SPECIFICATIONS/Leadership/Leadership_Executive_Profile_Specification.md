# Asterra Leadership / Executive Profile — Detailed Page Specification

**Package:** Package 3 — Professional Presence
**Template:** Template 01 — Corporate Authority
**Example Client:** Asterra Manufacturing Group
**Page:** Executive / Leadership Profile Detail
**Route:** `/leadership/[person-slug]`
**Version:** 1.0
**Status:** Design & implementation specification

> This specification follows the established Package 3 architecture, Universal Design Analysis, Template 01 — Corporate Authority, Asterra Design Manual, and the Asterra Leadership Listing specification. SEO requirements are based on the universal **SEO Analytics & Optimization Playbook**, applying only the relevant page-level requirements.

---

# 1. Page Purpose

The Executive Profile page provides deeper context about an individual member of Asterra's leadership.

The Leadership Listing answers:

> **Who leads Asterra?**

The Executive Profile answers:

> **Who is this leader, what responsibility do they hold, and what expertise do they bring to the organization?**

The page should establish credibility through **clear information and professional context**, not through exaggerated personal branding.

---

# 2. Page Objectives

The page should:

* Clearly identify the individual.
* Establish their role within Asterra.
* Explain their responsibilities.
* Present relevant professional experience.
* Connect the individual to Asterra's business.
* Provide appropriate expertise information.
* Link to related business areas where useful.
* Support organizational credibility.
* Provide a natural path back to the broader company.

# 5. Primary User Questions

The page should answer:

1. Who is this person?
2. What is their position?
3. What are they responsible for?
4. What experience is relevant to their role?
5. What areas do they specialize in?
6. Which Asterra business do they oversee?
7. How does their role connect to the wider organization?
8. Where can I learn more about Asterra?

# 10. Hero Content

### Eyebrow

`LEADERSHIP`

### H1

`[Full Name]`

### Position

`[Executive Position]`

# 11. Portrait

The portrait should follow the same art direction established on the Leadership Listing page.

Requirements:

* Consistent aspect ratio
* Consistent crop
* Professional photography
* High-quality source
* Appropriate responsive sizes
* Descriptive alt text

Avoid introducing a completely different photographic style on the detail page.

```text
┌──────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────────────┐
```

---

# 13. Section 02 — Profile Overview

## Objective

Provide the main biographical introduction.

### Structure

```text
PROFILE

Large editorial statement

Two or three concise paragraphs
```

The first paragraph should communicate the person's current role and relevance.

Additional paragraphs can explain:

* Career background
* Leadership focus
* Relevant industry experience
* Contribution to Asterra

---

# 14. Biography Rules

The biography should be:

* Factually accurate
* Relevant to the person's current role
* Concise enough for web reading
* Written in third person
* Free of exaggerated claims

Avoid turning the biography into a complete CV.

---

# 15. Section 03 — Responsibilities

## Objective

# 19. Section 05 — Areas of Expertise

# 23. Section 07 — Related Content

This section is optional.

Use it only when there is meaningful content associated with the person.

* Articles
* Interviews

* Industry perspectives


# 24. Section 08 — Leadership Navigation

The page should provide an easy way to move between leaders.

Recommended:

```text
← Previous Leader                     Next Leader →
```

And:

```text
[View All Leadership]
```

This should appear near the bottom of the profile.

The navigation should use the actual ordering established by the Leadership Listing.

---

# 25. Final CTA

A leadership profile does not necessarily need a sales-heavy CTA.

Preferred:

> **Explore what Asterra delivers.**

Actions:

**Explore Business Units**

**View Projects**

Alternatively:

> **Start a conversation with Asterra.**

**Contact Asterra**

The CTA should match the user's likely next step.

# 28. Color

Use the established Asterra palette.

Recommended:

**Warm Ivory**

Primary editorial background.

**White**

Content surfaces.

**Deep Evergreen**

Structural emphasis.

**Mineral Teal**

Links, actions, and subtle interaction states.

# 29. Motion

Motion should be restrained.

Possible:

* Portrait entrance
* Text reveal
* Subtle link movement
* Section reveal

Avoid:

* Animated biography text
* Rotating portrait effects
* Large profile transitions
* Excessive parallax
* Auto-changing profiles

---

# 30. Accessibility

Required:

* One H1
* Logical heading hierarchy
* Accessible breadcrumb
* Keyboard-accessible links
* Visible focus states
* Meaningful image alt text
* Sufficient contrast
* Accessible navigation between profiles
* Reduced-motion support


# 31. Content Integrity

This page requires strict factual control.

Never invent:

* Education
* Previous employers
* Professional certifications
* Awards
* Years of experience
* Job responsibilities
* Memberships
* Publications
* Professional achievements
* Quotes

Only approved information should be published.

---

# 32. Privacy

Only publish professional information appropriate for public corporate communication.

Do not include:

* Personal contact information
* Private addresses
* Personal phone numbers
* Personal information unrelated to the person's professional role

Use company contact routes instead.

# 41. Internal Linking

The profile should link to relevant:

* Leadership Listing
* Business Unit
* Capabilities
* Projects
* Insights
* Contact

Do not add links merely for SEO.

Every link should provide a meaningful navigation path.

---

# 42. Search Intent

Leadership profiles generally serve a mixture of:

* Navigational intent
* Informational intent
* Brand/entity research

The page should therefore prioritize **clear identity and factual information** rather than attempting to target unrelated commercial keywords.

---

# 43. Performance

Primary considerations:

* Optimize portrait images.
* Serve responsive image dimensions.
* Lazy-load below-fold imagery.
* Reserve image dimensions.
* Avoid unnecessary profile animations.
* Avoid loading unrelated content unnecessarily.

The portrait is important, but it should not become an unnecessarily heavy asset.

---

# 44. AI/Search Accessibility

Following the universal SEO playbook, the profile should make important entity information easy to understand.

Important facts should be expressed clearly in text:

```text
Name
Position
Organization
Responsibilities
Expertise
Business Unit
```

Do not hide essential information exclusively inside:

* Images
* Animations
* Hover states
* JavaScript-only interactions

This improves accessibility for users, search engines, and AI-driven search systems.

# 46. Anti-Patterns

Do not create:

* Generic social-media-style profiles
* Circular avatar-only layouts
* CV dumps
* Fake career achievements
* Fake credentials
* Excessive statistics
* Personal branding pages
* Large social media sections
* Department-specific color schemes
* Heavy animations
* Keyword-stuffed biographies
* Empty related-content sections
* Duplicate biography text across multiple pages

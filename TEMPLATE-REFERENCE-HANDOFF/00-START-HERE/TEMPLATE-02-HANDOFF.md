# Template 02 Handoff Guide: Editorial Expertise

**Template ID:** Template 02  
**Archetype:** Editorial Expertise  
**Core Direction:** Knowledge • Thought Leadership • Research Authority • Intellectual Impact  
**Target Industries:** Global management consultancies, economic think tanks, specialized legal practices, engineering research institutes, policy advisory firms, sustainability & ESG consultancies.

## 1. Executive Brief for Template 02 Designer

Template 02 shifts the visual and content emphasis from industrial factory scale (Template 01) to **intellectual rigor, research authority, and analytical clarity**. 

The user journey must feel like reading a high-prestige publication (e.g. *McKinsey Quarterly*, *The Economist*, *Financial Times Special Reports*, or *MIT Technology Review*) while maintaining corporate conversion pathways for high-value client advisory engagements.

## 3. Template 02 Design & UX Rules

1. **Typographic Rhythm & Hierarchy**:
   * Prioritize expressive, high-legibility editorial serif pairings (e.g. *Newsreader*, *Playfair*, *Lora*, *Merriweather*, or *Fraunces*) paired with clean neo-grotesque sans-serif for UI telemetry.
   * Provide generous paragraph leading (`line-height: 1.7 - 1.8`) and optimal reading measure (`60ch - 75ch`).
2. **Editorial Content Patterns**:
   * **Lead Monograph Spotlight**: Featured above-the-fold analysis with executive takeaways.
   * **Annotated Pull-Quotes**: Large serif quotes with attribution and subtle border accents.
   * **Key Takeaways Executive Strip**: Structured bullet points summarizing complex reports in under 60 seconds.
   * **Author & Peer Review Bylines**: Credential badges, contributor photos, and publication dates.
3. **Responsive Reading Comfort**:
   * Ensure effortless mobile reading with clean typography scaling and collapsible footnotes.
   * Sticky reading progress indicators and quick-navigation table of contents for long-form whitepapers.
